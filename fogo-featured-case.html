01 — The Challenge

More than the volcano.

How can an island shaped by a volcano be presented without reducing it to the volcano itself?

Fogo combines black volcanic landscapes with agriculture, historic architecture, mountain communities and everyday life. The project set out to translate that range into a cinematic visual narrative while keeping the island recognisable and grounded in local reference.

The aim was to show what is already there, not to reinvent the island.

Strategic focus

Present Fogo through the relationship between its volcanic terrain and the life around it: cultivation, architecture, communities and the coast.

Project scope

Reference research, creative direction, a set of AI-assisted visual studies and a 39-second vertical film.

Evidence standard

Public municipal and island sources for context. Generated scenes are labelled as interpretation, and no commercial results are claimed.

02 — The Insight

Authenticity became the strategy.

Fogo’s identity comes from the relationship between land, communities, cultivation, architecture and the Atlantic. The material for the story already existed and did not need to be invented.

This changed the role of AI in the project. Instead of producing a more dramatic island, each visual decision had to stay connected to recognisable local reference. AI could support light, composition and atmosphere; local reference remained the filter.

03 — The Concept

SHAPED BY FIRE.
DEFINED BY LIFE.

The volcano formed the island’s landscape. The concept places equal weight on the people, crops and towns that live within it.

04 — Creative Direction

Cinematic realism grounded in local reference.

Guiding principle: elevated in presentation, believable in place.

AI supported the cinematic interpretation. Local reference guided the creative decisions.

05 — Reference & Creative Decisions

Public reference set the boundaries.

The goal was not only to find attractive references but to keep Fogo recognisable. Each study was checked against one question: could this realistically belong to Fogo?

Reference → Generate → Compare → Refine → Review

Documented context

The municipality of Mosteiros describes coffee cultivation on volcanic soils at elevations of 350–1,300 metres. This gives the coffee theme a specific geographic context. Read the municipal source ↗

Creative risk

A lush, generic tropical landscape could obscure the relationship between cultivation and volcanic terrain.

Visual criteria

The coffee study is read through three elements: cultivation in the foreground, mountain relief and settlement in the distance. These are creative criteria, not a geographic survey.

Interpretation

The image is an AI-assisted visual study. It does not establish the exact location of a plantation or document a real viewpoint.

06 — The Production

AI assisted the production. Human direction shaped the result.

Reference → Prompt → Generate → Evaluate → Refine → Approve

Each generated scene was evaluated for believability before visual impact. Scenes that looked striking but did not read as Fogo were refined or left out.

07 — The Edit

The final story was built in the edit.

Individual scenes were connected through rhythm, sequence, contrast and sound. The studies below show the visual range explored across the project; the film presents the selected sequence.

The sequence moves from the volcanic landscape to the life that exists around it.
