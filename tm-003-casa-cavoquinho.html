Stay in the Rhythm of the Valley.

A hospitality creative study built around the guest journey through Paul Valley.

Independent Creative Case Study · Timas Motion. Not commissioned by or officially affiliated with Casa Cavoquinho.

01 — The Challenge

More than a place to stay.

Casa Cavoquinho already presents the practical side of a stay: accommodation, breakfast and dinner, hiking information and access to the Paul Valley.

The brief was not to invent a new experience, or to suggest that the property’s own communication was falling short. It was to explore how those existing elements could connect into a clearer guest journey, so a prospective visitor understands what the property offers and also what staying there could feel like.

How can a rural guesthouse be presented not simply as accommodation in the valley, but as a base for experiencing the valley itself?

02 — The Insight

The journey became the story.

The opportunity was in connecting the moments of the stay, rather than listing them.

  1. Wake in the valley.
  2. Move through it.
  3. Experience the landscape.
  4. Return to the same base.
  5. Slow down.

03 — The Strategy

The house is the base.
The valley is the experience.

Objective

Turn the property’s existing experiences into a clear guest journey that helps prospective visitors picture Casa Cavoquinho as their base in the Paul Valley.

Core promise

A base from which guests experience the valley, return to comfort, and stay connected to the place throughout their visit.

Logic

The property is the base. The valley is the experience. The guest connects the two.

Narrative

wake → prepare → explore → return → rest

Casa → Valley → Casa

04 — The Big Idea

STAY IN THE RHYTHM
OF THE VALLEY.

“Stay” ties the line to hospitality. “Rhythm” carries the pace of the experience: waking in the valley, moving through it, coming back and slowing down.

The valley stopped being the setting and became the rhythm of the stay.

05 — Creative Direction

Feel a day in the valley.
Don’t watch an ad about it.

North star: make the viewer feel a day in the Paul Valley, not watch an advertisement about it.

  1. Presence over spectacle.
  2. The guest journey stays central.
  3. Local reality is the filter.
  4. Sensory continuity.

Inside the guesthouse

Closer, calmer, human shots.

Outside

Broader landscapes and movement through the valley.

Return

Intimate again.

AI supports the cinematic interpretation. Reality and public visual references guide the decisions.

06 — Research & Reality Framework

Reality set the boundaries.
Direction worked inside them.

The project drew on Casa Cavoquinho’s published information and on public visual references to the Paul Valley.

About the stay

  • Guesthouse context
  • Valley-facing accommodation
  • Breakfast and dinner
  • Hiking information
  • How the stay relates to the valley

About the valley

  • General topography
  • Cultivated landscapes
  • Rural pathways and scale
  • Buildings on steep terrain

Interpretation

The generated house, room, breakfast setup and guest scenes are conceptual interpretations. They are not documentary records and do not show the property’s exact architecture, interiors, food presentation or guest experience.

What this case does not claim

No booking growth, occupancy change, revenue impact, client approval or commercial performance.

07 — The Production

Nine moments.
One guest journey.

The film was designed as one connected journey, not a set of separate hospitality shots.

  1. 01Morning Presence
  2. 02Wake / First Contact
  3. 03Breakfast / Local Taste
  4. 04Preparation to Leave
  5. 05Into the Valley
  6. 06Through the Valley
  7. 07The Return
  8. 08Late-Afternoon Rest
  9. 09Closing / The Promise
Generate for the story → Validate against references → Correct for continuity

Every scene had to earn its place in the journey.

08 — The Edit

Calm, movement, calm.

calm → preparation → exploration → return → rest

The opening is restrained. The middle section carries more movement. The ending slows down again. Sound and music hold the spaces and the stages of the day together.
